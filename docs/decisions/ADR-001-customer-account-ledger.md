# ADR-001: Customer Account Ledger

## Status
Proposed - Pending client confirmation.

## Context
Customers may:
- Buy multiple times on credit.
- Pay irregular amounts.
- Make partial payments.
- Return products.
- Continue buying while an outstanding balance exists.

Tying every receipt to one sales invoice would add unnecessary complexity to the current workflow.

## Decision
Use a customer-level account ledger.

CustomerAccountEntries records debit and credit movements.

- Sales invoices increase customer debit.
- Invoice payments decrease customer debit.
- Later customer receipts decrease customer debit.
- Returns reduce customer debt.
- Refunds are recorded through the return workflow.

Receipts are not assigned to individual invoices in V2.

## Consequences
- Customer balance is calculated from ledger entries.
- Customer statements show the complete movement history.
- Per-invoice outstanding balances are not available in V2.
- FIFO payment allocation is deferred.
- Ledger entries are generated by business operations, not edited directly.
